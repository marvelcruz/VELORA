THE BESPOKE PROCESS

Five steps from idea to final fitting.

  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    We define your occasion, style preferences, wardrobe needs and the way you want the garment to feel.

  2. 02

    Select your fabric

    Choose cloth based on texture, climate, weight, color, pattern and how formal the finished piece should be.

  3. 03

    Style the garment

    Choose lapels, buttons, lining, stitching, pockets, cuffs and other personal details.

  4. 04

    Get measured

    Detailed measurements account for body proportions, posture and the intended fit.

  5. 05

    Fitting & final touches

    The finished garment is reviewed on-body and adjusted where needed for comfort, balance and finish.

TAILORING HERITAGE

Three generations of craft.

1956

Sita Ram Chauhan begins working as a custom-suit tailor.

1964

Sunshine Tailors is established in Delhi.

1978

Anil Kumar begins training under Sita Ram Chauhan.

1984

The tailoring business expands to a second store.

2012

Rivaado is established by third-generation tailor Manuj Chauhan.

2016

The business expands across India in the B2B market.

2022

The bespoke operation expands to Calgary.
